# Resonance Report

Rewritten on the 1st of each month by the `prm-resonance` agent (see
`Agents/prm-resonance.md`) — which modes are landing with which people,
aggregated from every `Interactions/*.md` note's `resonance` score. Every
rewrite lands as a proposal in your Review queue.

`resonance` scores the *engagement*, not the person — this report is meant
to change how you spend time with people, not to grade them.

_No run yet — the first report lands after a full month with the desktop
app opened at least once. Until then, `Bases/High Resonance.base` shows
every interaction logged at resonance ≥ 4, live._
